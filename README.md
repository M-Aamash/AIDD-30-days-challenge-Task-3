## 1. What new improvements were introduced in Gemini 3.0?

### Gemini 3.0 reasoning, coding, aur multimodal (image/video) understanding ko bohot improve karta hai.Saath hi Deep Think mode, 1M context window aur faster + safer performance introduce ki gayi hai.

## 2. How does Gemini 3.0 improve coding & automation workflows?

### Gemini 3.0 coding ko automate kar deta hai kyunki ab AI editor, terminal aur browser ko use karke multi-step coding tasks khud complete kar sakta hai isme improved planning aur execution hai jo complex workflows ko tezi aur accuracy ke sath handle karta hai.

## 3. How does Gemini 3.0 improve multimodal understanding?

### Gemini 3.0 images, video, audio aur documents ko bohut behtar samajhta hai — na sirf OCR, balkay “visual reasoning” aur spatial understanding me bhi naya level laya gaya hai. Uska video reasoning “high-frame-rate” samajh sakta hai aur lambi footage me detail recall kar sakta hai.
